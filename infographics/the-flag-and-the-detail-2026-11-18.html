The Flag and the Detail

Data Dictionary 2.1 has 105 yes/no (Boolean) fields, counted once per resource. 103 of them are named with a YN ending and are the ones studied here. For 46 of the 103, one or more fields on the same resource carry the detail the yes or no summarizes: GarageYN and GarageSpaces, WaterfrontYN and WaterfrontFeatures. Five Kinds of Partner

How the partner relates to the yes/no field, across all 103 fields.

14Feature listThe partner lists the specifics: WaterfrontYN and WaterfrontFeatures.
12CountThe partner counts it: GarageYN and GarageSpaces.
16Several fieldsTwo or more fields each carry part of the detail: FireplaceYN with FireplacesTotal and FireplaceFeatures.
4Description or typeA text field or a pick list of types describes it: AdditionalParcelsYN and AdditionalParcelsDescription (text), ConciergeServiceYN and ConciergeServiceType (a pick list).
57Stands aloneNo other field on the resource carries its detail: CompSaleYN and HomeWarrantyYN.

Source. Industry Aggregates, November 18, 2026, the latest report per market and system: 432 markets across 29 providers. Every count is out of those totals.

Counting. A market or provider counts toward the detail when it sends any of the pair's partner fields and toward the flag when it sends the yes/no field. A provider counts once, whichever of its markets sends the field, so its two sides can come from different markets. These are markets and providers reached, not listings.

Partners are the field or fields that carry the detail a yes/no field summarizes, matched by definition, one resource at a time. A field on two resources counts once on each, so AssociationYN on Property and on Building are separate rows with their own partner lists. The lists name the closest partners and are not exhaustive. Two wider readings were measured. Adding AssociationFeeFrequency, AssociationFeeIncludes, AssociationPhone and AssociationPhone2 to the partners of AssociationYN on Property raises its detail from 375 markets to 383. The detail leads either way. Counting ParkingFeatures, a feature list that can name a garage, as a partner of GarageYN raises its detail from 342 markets to 398, which would move that pair from the flag side (347 markets) to the detail side: 18 of 24 in place of 17.

Stands alone means no other field on the resource carries the flag's detail. A value in a list can still say the same thing. Lookup values are outside this study. The clearest case: none of the 432 markets sends FireSprinklerSystemYN. 240 of them send the Fire Sprinkler System value of SecurityFeatures.

Fields studied. The 103 fields are every Data Dictionary 2.1 Boolean field named with a YN ending. The other 2 Boolean fields, OUID.OrganizationStatus and Showing.ShowingAllowed, are named differently and are not part of the study. Every field name on this page opens that field in the Data Dictionary.

Not measured means no market's data in this run includes the field's resource.
