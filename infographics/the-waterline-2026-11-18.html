Source. Industry Aggregates, November 18, 2026 run. 427 markets across 28 providers send Property data; shares are of those, except the WaterfrontFeatures values, which are of the 309 markets that send that field. Each market counts once, from its latest certification report.

Counting. A market is counted when its data sends any element that carries the idea, standard or local. The aggregates show markets per value, not listings, so they cannot say how many listings have more than one body of water.

Where it lands follows the WaterBody Shape sheet and the Data Dictionary 3.0 deprecations sheet. "Lands with loss" means the idea lands only in part or on a nearest value. "Stays on Property" means a field the Shape does not replace.
