What WaterfrontFeatures Becomes

The 20 standard values of WaterfrontFeatures, as a share of the 309 markets that send the field. Of these, 4 are defined "on or near" the water. The deprecations sheet lists WaterfrontYN among their replacements. Because the definition does not say which, RESO reads that flag as unknown for them and shows them as landing with loss. Each bar counts the value's display name and its Legacy OData value, which are one value. Markets that send a value only in another spelling are not in the bar: 5 values have some, noted beside the name and listed apart in the table. Each value opens its page in the Data Dictionary.

Source. Industry Aggregates, November 18, 2026 run. 427 markets across 28 providers send Property data; shares are of those, except the WaterfrontFeatures values, which are of the 309 markets that send that field. Each market counts once, from its latest certification report. The Building Resource is for the real property itself, and listings carry that information in the Property Resource, which is the one counted here.

Counting. A market is counted when its data sends any element that carries the idea, standard or local. Water body name is the one exception: it counts only the 130 of the 427 markets that send the WaterBodyName field, because a name sent in a field of a market's own cannot be told apart by a rule a reader could check. The aggregates show markets per value, not listings, so they cannot say how many listings have more than one body of water.

Where it lands follows the Data Dictionary Workgroup's WaterBody Shape sheet and its Data Dictionary 3.0 deprecations sheet, as they stood on November 17, 2026. Both are working drafts. Data Dictionary 3.0 is not ratified. The Shape's fields and values are not in Data Dictionary 2.1 and may change. "Lands with loss" means the idea lands only in part or on a nearest value, and which value is nearest is RESO's reading, not the sheet's. "Stays on Property" means a field the Shape does not replace, such as WaterfrontYN. In the bars and the legend each status has its own texture as well as its own color.
