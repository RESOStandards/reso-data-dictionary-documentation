In the Data, in No Proposal Yet

Ways markets describe mitigation that neither the approved nor the proposed values name.

  • Tie-downs on manufactured homes 34 markets
  • Storm protection, no type named 10 markets
  • Firewise community 4 markets
  • Wind mitigation inspection or certificate 2 markets
  • FORTIFIED, no level named 1 market

Source. Industry Aggregates, November 18, 2026 run. 427 markets across 28 providers send Property data, and every share is of those. Each market counts once, from its latest certification report.

Counting. A market counts for a value when it sends it in any spelling, as a list value or as a field of its own, in any field. The aggregates show markets per value, not listings. Free-text remarks are not counted. Neither are storm windows and doors, shutters that do not say they are for storms, hazard zones or insurance.

Status. Approved values have passed a Data Dictionary Workgroup vote and are not yet in a ratified version, so every market that sends one today sends a local value. Proposed values are under discussion by the Workgroup. Fire Sprinkler System has been a standard SecurityFeatures value since Data Dictionary 1.7. IBHS is the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety.
