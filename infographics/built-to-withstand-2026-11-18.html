Source. Industry Aggregates, November 18, 2026 run. 427 markets across 28 providers send Property data, and every share is of those. Each market counts once, from its latest certification report.

Counting. A market counts for a value when it sends it in any spelling, as a list value or as a field of its own, in any field. The aggregates show markets per value, not listings. Free-text remarks are not counted. Neither are storm windows and doors, hazard zones or insurance.

Other wordings. One rule holds for every value. A market counts for a value when it sends the value's own words, in any order or form, or the words the Workgroup's proposal gives as its equal: storm shutters for Hurricane Shutters, impact resistant glass for Impact Glass and impact resistant shingles for Impact Resistant. Any other wording of the same feature is counted on its own and shown under the value, never added to it. Shutters. Shutters is an existing Data Dictionary 2.1 value of WindowFeatures. It names no hazard, so it is neither a spelling nor another wording of Hurricane Shutters and it is in no count on this page. 61 of the 427 markets send it there, and 51 of those send no storm shutter wording of any kind.

A field of their own. A market is counted in that tile when it sends a field it defined itself, in Property or among the custom fields it sends beside Property, that is about storm protection or disaster mitigation as a whole. A field for a single feature, such as a shutters field, is not counted. 8 of the 9 send it among their custom fields and 2 in Property, 1 of them in both.
