Two sibling fields, two different lists

CommunityFeatures names pickleball. AssociationAmenities does not, and 27 markets report it there. Basketball and shuffleboard are the mirror image: standard on AssociationAmenities, absent from CommunityFeatures, where 20 and 17 markets report them.

On AssociationAmenities, pickleball and shuffleboard each reach 27 markets. Only shuffleboard has a standard value there.

Sport court is the broad answer

Sport Court already covers the generic case and the Duplication Subgroup has approved it. It is standard on AssociationAmenities, where 33 markets report it, and it is reported on ExteriorFeatures and CommunityFeatures, which do not name it.

Whether a named sport should be its own value or roll up to the generic one is the question the list above puts to the group.

Golf is the only sport with a place

LotFeatures names On Golf Course and Near Golf Course, and View names Golf Course. No other sport is named on either field, and markets report tennis on both: 5 markets on LotFeatures, including the wording "On Tennis Court", and 4 on View.

Sports land on fields for what they cost

Tennis is reported on AssociationFeeIncludes by 28 markets and on RentIncludes by 7, more than it reaches on most feature fields. Neither names a sport. What the fee or the rent buys is a separate question from what the property has.

RESO Industry Aggregates, June 2026 run. Each count is the markets reporting that sport on that field, measured against the markets that report the field at all, which differs by field. Every figure was checked against the December 2025 run and held.

Standard values are Data Dictionary 2.1. A value's display form and its machine form are one value, so TennisCourts is not counted as a variation of Tennis Court(s). Spelling variants are grouped by concept: pickleball is reported in four spellings on CommunityFeatures, one of which is the standard value.

The chips show fields with two or more markets. A further 28 sport and field pairs sit at a single market each, among them tennis on SecurityFeatures, PetsAllowed and ArchitecturalStyle, which look like reporting mistakes rather than vocabulary. Croquet is the fourteenth sport and appears only at that single-market level, so it has no row above. Free-text and area-name enumerations are left out, since a sport named inside a remark or a subdivision name is not a vocabulary choice, and so is BusinessType, where a batting cage is a kind of business rather than an amenity.
