Standard forms: markets sending the value's display name or its Legacy OData value exactly, which are one value. Other spellings only: markets counted for the kind that send neither standard form. The two add up to Markets.

Source. Industry Aggregates, November 18, 2026, the latest report per market and system. Property.Flooring is sent by 371 markets across 23 providers; every share is of those. A market is counted when its data sends a spelling that names the kind. The aggregates carry no per-value record counts, so these are markets reached, not listings.

Counting rule. Each value is split into parts at commas, slashes, semicolons, ampersands, plus signs, bars, parentheses and the word and. Run-together names are read as separate words. Case and other punctuation are ignored. A part counts for one wood kind, the first of these that its words satisfy: Simulated Wood (simulated or faux with wood, or wood look or wood like); Laminate (laminate or laminated); Engineered Hardwood (engineered alone or with wood, hardwood or flooring); Reclaimed Wood (reclaimed with wood); FSC or SFI Certified Source Hardwood (FSC or SFI); Hardwood (hardwood, hardwoods or hard wood); Softwood (softwood or soft wood); Wood (the word wood). A part that says subfloor counts for none of them. A part also counts for Parquet, Bamboo, Cork, Pine or Plywood when it holds that word. So a value naming carpet and hardwood counts for Hardwood, wood laminate counts for Laminate and not for Wood, hard surface is not Hardwood and a wood subfloor is not Wood. Abbreviations and misspellings are not expanded: no market in this run sends Hardwood only that way. A market counts once for a kind when any of its values has a part that counts for it.

Groups. Bamboo and Cork are grouped with the wood values on this page. Each is its own Data Dictionary value. Laminate and Simulated Wood are kept apart: each is its own Data Dictionary value, and sending one does not say the floor is wood. Pine and Plywood are proposed values: they come from RESO's Universal Mapping Initiative analysis of this field and have been put to a RESO group. They are not Data Dictionary values. They are counted by the same rule as the rest, on the word pine and the word plywood.

Scope. Only Property.Flooring is counted. 24 markets also send flooring room by room in a field of their own, and some send a yes or no field per material. Those are not counted here. Wood floors also turn up in other standard fields: 28 of the 427 markets that send Property data name a wood, bamboo, parquet or laminate floor in a standard field other than Flooring, most often InteriorFeatures (13 markets), and 8 of the 28 do not send Flooring at all. They are not counted here either. Every share on this page is of the 371 markets that send Flooring, so no figure above depends on them. Building.Flooring, the other field that uses this lookup, is not measured: the run holds no Building data. Each Data Dictionary value above opens its page in the Data Dictionary.
