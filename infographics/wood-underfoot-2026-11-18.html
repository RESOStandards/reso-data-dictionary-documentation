Source. Industry Aggregates, November 18, 2026, the latest report per market and system. Property.Flooring is sent by 371 markets across 23 providers; every share is of those. A market is counted when its data sends a spelling that names the kind. The aggregates carry no per-value record counts, so these are markets reached, not listings.

Spellings are matched as in the Flooring analysis: exact and normalized forms, combined values split by kind, "… Floors" suffixes and the "Engineered Wood" family mapped to their standard values.

Laminate and Simulated Wood look like wood and are not wood. Pine and Plywood are proposed values, counted the same way as the rest: over the spellings the Flooring analysis proposed for them. Spellings first seen after that analysis are counted only in the species rows.
